# Dog breeds

**Расе паса** contains 40 familiar or visually distinctive breeds, with Serbian Cyrillic labels and the prompt **Које је расе овај пас?**. The game uses the same configurable 30-question random selection, four shuffled answers, feedback, scoring and separate resume entry as the other quizzes.

This pack is more detailed than the preschool animal pack. A single breed photo is an example, not a promise that every dog of that breed has the same color or coat. Poodle sizes, schnauzer sizes and coat varieties are not separate answer choices; the corgi entry uses the familiar broad name. The cocker-spaniel photo represents an English cocker spaniel, and the Akita entry represents the Japanese breed. No mixed breeds, breed crosses or separate American Akita entry are included. The general animal quiz continues to label a dog simply **Пас**.

Full photos fit inside the question area without additional cropping. Photos are bundled in `web/dogs/`; source files, authors and per-image license metadata are stored in `dog-sources.json`. The shared credits page is built by `python3 scripts/build_credits.py` and linked from the footer. Preserve the source links, attribution and image licenses when redistributing.
